# About video — Drake's Pricing

Status: script draft, not reviewed/approved. Do not produce or publish
final video assets from this script without sign-off.

## Purpose

Explain why a standard CMA is insufficient, and how Drake's Pricing
combines comparable sales, current competition, market velocity,
absorption, failed listings, buyer response, property condition, and
pricing behavior into an executable pricing strategy — not just a
historical value estimate.

## Core message

> "Comparable sales tell you what happened. Drake's Pricing is designed
> to help determine what the market is likely to do next."

## 60–90 second version

1. **(0:00–0:08) Open on the problem.** A CMA printout, static and dated.
   VO: "A comparable-sales report tells you what already sold. It doesn't
   tell you what happens next."
2. **(0:08–0:20) Introduce the input.** Property address entered into
   Drake's Pricing. VO: "Drake's Pricing starts the same way — with the
   property. But it doesn't stop at comps."
3. **(0:20–0:40) Show the layers.** Quick cuts: comp selection criteria,
   market velocity chart, absorption rate, failed/expired listings nearby,
   buyer-response signal. VO: "It reads current competition, how fast this
   price band is moving, where offers are actually landing — and where
   they stop."
4. **(0:40–0:55) Dead-zone visualization.** Price band chart with a
   highlighted "buyer resistance" zone. VO: "That's a pricing dead zone —
   a band where buyer interest measurably drops off. Most tools can't see
   it. Drake's Pricing is built to find it."
5. **(0:55–1:15) The output.** Recommended list price, confidence range,
   and a one-line rationale on screen. VO: "The result isn't just a
   number — it's a strategy: where to launch, what to expect, and why."
6. **(1:15–1:25) Close on the core message.** VO: "Comparable sales tell
   you what happened. Drake's Pricing is designed to help you see what's
   likely to happen next." Logo + CTA: "Run a Pricing Analysis."

## 20–30 second landing-page cut

1. **(0:00–0:06)** CMA printout → property input. VO: "A CMA tells you
   what already sold."
2. **(0:06–0:16)** Quick flashes: competition, velocity, dead-zone
   highlight, recommended price. VO: "Drake's Pricing reads the market
   itself — competition, momentum, and where buyer interest drops off —
   to recommend a price and a strategy, not just an estimate."
3. **(0:16–0:24)** Recommended price + confidence on screen. VO: "Know
   what the market is likely to do next." Logo + CTA: "Run a Pricing
   Analysis."

## Constraints for whoever produces this

- Every on-screen number must be clearly labeled as illustrative/sample
  data, never a specific real client's report, unless legal has cleared
  using a real (consented) example.
- No claim of guaranteed sale price, guaranteed time-to-sell, or
  guaranteed accuracy percentage. "Likely," "designed to help," and
  ranges — not promises.
- Do not show or reference "Pending without reduction" or any other
  internal/admin-only metric on screen; it is not a public-facing figure.
