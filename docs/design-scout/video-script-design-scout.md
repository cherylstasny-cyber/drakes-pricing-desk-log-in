# About video — Design Scout

Status: script draft, not reviewed/approved. Do not produce or publish
final video assets from this script without sign-off. This replaces a
single generalized about-video approach with a dedicated video for this
product line (paired with `video-script-pricing.md` for Drake's Pricing).

## Opening line

> "Your buyers don't really search for four bedrooms and three bathrooms.
> They search for the house they can picture themselves living in."

## Core message

> "Traditional alerts search the information entered into the MLS. Search
> by Design analyzes the property itself."

## Closing line

> "Stop asking buyers to search the inventory. Let Design Scout watch
> the market for them."

## 60–90 second version

1. **(0:00–0:10) Cold open on the line above,** over a slow pan across a
   living room with distinctive architectural character (beamed ceiling,
   warm wood). VO delivers the opening line.
2. **(0:10–0:28) State the problem.** Split screen: an MLS search form
   with checkboxes (beds/baths/price) vs. a client's actual wish list read
   aloud — "Frank Lloyd Wright-inspired, white exterior, warm wood kitchen,
   Sub-Zero appliances, a glass refrigerated wine room, a media room, a
   freestanding tub." VO: "Most of what a buyer actually wants was never a
   searchable field."
3. **(0:28–0:45) Introduce the product.** Agent typing a natural-language
   description into Design Scout's "Describe the home your client is
   waiting for" box. VO: "The agent describes exactly what the client
   wants. Design Scout converts it into a structured watch — must
   have, preferred, and avoid."
4. **(0:45–1:05) Show the monitoring loop.** New listing arrives → photos
   and remarks analyzed → traits stored with evidence → compared against
   active watches. VO: "Every new listing is read the same way — photos,
   remarks, and history — and compared against what your clients are
   actually waiting for."
5. **(1:05–1:20) The alert.** Buyer's phone: match email with photo, match
   percentage, top matching traits, "Request a Showing." Agent's screen:
   "New 93% match sent to Susan Smith." VO: core message line.
6. **(1:20–1:30) Close** on the closing line + logo + CTA: "Create a
   Design Scout."

## 20–30 second landing-page cut

1. **(0:00–0:06)** Opening line over the same architectural pan.
2. **(0:06–0:16)** Fast cuts: NL description typed → structured
   must/prefer/avoid list → new listing photo → match alert on a phone.
   VO: core message line.
3. **(0:16–0:24)** Closing line + logo + CTA: "Create a Design Scout."

## Constraints for whoever produces this

- Never claim a property "was designed by" a named architect; use
  "Wright-inspired" / "Prairie influence" language only, matching the
  taxonomy's own evidence rules.
- Any brand name shown on screen (Sub-Zero, Wolf, etc.) must be paired
  with a "likely" or "confirmed" treatment consistent with the product's
  actual confidence model — don't show a confident brand claim the real
  product wouldn't make.
- The sample match score, listing, and client name used must be clearly
  fictional/synthetic unless a real, consenting client and listing have
  been cleared for use.
- Do not depict or reference photo sources the company doesn't have
  confirmed rights to use (see `DATA_SOURCE_ABSTRACTION.md`).
